## 📽️ SLIDE 2: The Problem
### **The $500 Billion Secondary Marketplace Crisis**
1. **The Cash & Fee Trap**: Platforms like eBay, StockX, and Poshmark charge 10%–15% in seller and payment fees, cutting directly into user margins.
2. **2D Visual Limitations**: Flat photos cannot communicate real-world scale, ergonomics, surface scratches, or device depth, leading to 30% return rates and disputes.
3. **Impersonal Negotiations**: Chat-based haggling is adversarial, slow, and prone to ghosting.
4. **E-Waste & Idleness**: Over $1.2 Trillion in functional consumer electronics sits idle in households worldwide.

---

## 📽️ SLIDE 3: The Solution
### **Xchange Spatial: Spatial Computing Meets Circular Economy**
- **100% Cashless Barter**: Users trade gear they already own for gear they want without spending fiat currency.
- **Volumetric 3D Dual-Pan Scale**: A physical-scale 3D scale floats in the user's room. Placing items onto the virtual pans tilts the scale in 3D space, showing real-time trade parity.
- **3D Spatial Hologram Inspection**: Examine camera lenses, keyboards, and synthesizers at 1:1 true scale before committing.
- **Shared Spatial Negotiation Lounge**: Two traders enter an immersive virtual lounge to negotiate live with spatial audio cues.

## 📽️ SLIDE 10: Viva Defense Q&A Preparation

### Expected Examiner Questions & Model Answers:
- **Q1: Why choose visionOS over tvOS for Part B?**
  - *Answer*: visionOS unlocks true 3D spatial computing, depth perception, and collaborative immersive spaces. Testing physical balance scales and inspecting camera lenses in 1:1 physical scale is uniquely suited to Apple Vision Pro, whereas tvOS is restricted to remote-controlled 2D media consumption.
- **Q2: How does the application ensure user safety during physical meetups?**
  - *Answer*: Through our MapKit Safe Exchange Zone system. The app guides users exclusively to verified, well-lit, CCTV-monitored locations such as university campus security desks and police station barter stalls.
- **Q3: What prevents users from cheating on item valuations?**
  - *Answer*: Our built-in CoreML and Apple Vision Appraisal Service analyzes photos to establish algorithmic baseline market values and condition multipliers, providing an impartial, transparent benchmark.
